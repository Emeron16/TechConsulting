# VarSaver — Month-to-Month Variable Rate Plan (Texas)

## 1. Plan Overview

VarSaver is a month-to-month, no-contract electricity plan available to
residential customers in ERCOT service territories. The per-kWh energy
charge is variable and may change monthly based on wholesale market
conditions, with 30 days' advance notice provided to the customer before
any rate change takes effect.

## 2. Rate Structure

- **Base charge:** $9.95 per billing cycle
- **Energy charge:** Variable, set monthly; published on the customer's
  bill and on the rate history page of the customer portal
- **No early termination fee** — since there is no fixed contract term,
  customers may switch providers at any time without penalty

## 3. Contract Term

None. VarSaver auto-renews monthly and continues indefinitely until the
customer switches plans or providers. There is no minimum commitment
period.

## 4. Usage Credits and Bill Credits

VarSaver does **not** include a winter usage credit, seasonal bill credit,
or any usage-tier credit of any kind. Customers seeking a winter usage
credit should be directed to PLAN-FIXED24-TX, which includes one, or to
PLAN-FREENIGHTS-TX depending on their usage pattern.

## 5. Billing and Payment

Standard monthly billing applies. See BILL-LATEFEE-POLICY for late payment
terms and BILL-AUTOPAY-DISCOUNT for available billing discounts (VarSaver
customers remain eligible for the autopay discount).
