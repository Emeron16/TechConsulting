# Autopay and Paperless Billing Discount Terms

## 1. Discount Overview

Customers enrolled in **both** autopay (automatic monthly payment via a
saved bank account or card) **and** paperless billing (email delivery of
statements instead of mail) receive a **$5.00 monthly bill credit**,
applied automatically to the following month's statement.

## 2. Eligibility

- Available on all active residential plans, including PLAN-FIXED24-TX,
  PLAN-VAR-TX, and PLAN-FREENIGHTS-TX
- Enrollment in only one of the two programs (autopay-only or
  paperless-only) does **not** qualify for the discount — both are
  required together
- Commercial accounts are not eligible for this residential discount

## 3. Enrollment and Removal

Enrollment takes effect on the next billing cycle after both programs are
active on the account. If a customer disables autopay or paperless billing
at any point, the discount is removed starting the following billing
cycle — it is not prorated mid-cycle and no credit is clawed back for
prior months.

## 4. Interaction with Other Discounts

The autopay/paperless discount stacks with plan-level pricing (it is a
flat bill credit, not a rate adjustment) but does not stack with any other
promotional bill-credit offer active on the same account at the same time;
the higher of the two credits applies if both would otherwise qualify.
