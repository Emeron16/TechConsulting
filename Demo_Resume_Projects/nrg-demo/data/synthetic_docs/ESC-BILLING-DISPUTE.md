# Billing Dispute Escalation Playbook

## 1. Purpose

Guides support agents on when and how to escalate a customer's billing
dispute internally, versus resolving it directly at the first point of
contact.

## 2. Resolve at First Contact (No Escalation Needed)

An agent may resolve the following directly, without escalation:

- Disputed late fee where the customer's payment was received before the
  due date per the payment processor's timestamp (waive the fee, per
  BILL-LATEFEE-POLICY §4)
- Autopay/paperless discount not applied when both programs are
  confirmed active (apply the credit manually, per BILL-AUTOPAY-DISCOUNT)
- Simple billing question about current-cycle usage or rate (answer
  directly using account/plan data)

## 3. Escalate to Billing Disputes Team

Escalate to the Billing Disputes team (via internal ticket, not a customer
transfer) when:

- The customer disputes a charge of **$100 or more** on a single
  statement
- The customer alleges metering error (usage reading appears
  implausible relative to their historical pattern)
- The dispute involves more than one billing cycle
- The customer explicitly requests a formal dispute be opened (Texas
  regulations require this be honored regardless of the agent's own
  assessment of the dispute's merits)

## 4. Escalate to Compliance/Legal

Escalate directly to Compliance/Legal (do not attempt to resolve at the
agent level) when:

- The dispute involves an allegation that the plan's advertised terms did
  not match the EFL disclosure (a potential PUCT disclosure violation)
- The customer references a regulatory complaint already filed with PUCT

## 5. Timeline Requirements

Once escalated, the Billing Disputes team must acknowledge the customer
within 3 business days and resolve or provide a status update within 10
business days, per internal SLA (independent of any external regulatory
timeline that may also apply).

- BILL-AUTOPAY-DISCOUNT: Autopay/paperless discount terms
