# FreeNights — Time-of-Use Plan with Free Nights (Texas)

## 1. Plan Overview

FreeNights is a 12-month, time-of-use residential electricity plan for
ERCOT service territories. Energy usage between **9:00 PM and 6:00 AM**
local time is billed at $0.00 per kWh ("free nights"). Usage during all
other hours is billed at the standard daytime rate.

## 2. Rate Structure

- **Base charge:** $9.95 per billing cycle
- **Daytime energy charge (6:00 AM–9:00 PM):** $0.14 per kWh
- **Nighttime energy charge (9:00 PM–6:00 AM):** $0.00 per kWh

## 3. Contract Term

12 months. Early termination fee of $150 applies if the customer switches
providers before the contract term ends, prorated to $0 at the 12-month
mark.

## 4. Usage Credits

No separate winter usage credit applies to this plan; the free-nights
structure itself is the primary savings mechanism, year-round.
