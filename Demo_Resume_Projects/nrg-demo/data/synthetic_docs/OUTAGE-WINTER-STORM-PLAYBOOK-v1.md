# Extreme Weather Outage Playbook (Winter Storm / Grid Emergency)

## 1. Purpose

Following Winter Storm Uri (February 2021) and the resulting scrutiny of
how Texas retail electricity providers communicate with customers during
grid emergencies, this playbook governs customer communication whenever
ERCOT declares a grid-level emergency condition (EEA1/EEA2/EEA3) or PUCT
issues emergency guidance. This playbook **supersedes**
OUTAGE-COMMS-PROTOCOL's standard timelines for the duration of the
declared emergency.

## 2. Activation

This playbook is activated automatically when ERCOT publishes an Energy
Emergency Alert (EEA) of any level, or manually by NRG's Emergency
Operations lead. All support agents receive an internal notification when
activated.

## 3. Communication Differences from Standard Protocol

| Item | Standard Protocol | Emergency Playbook |
|---|---|---|
| ERT guidance | Published within 2 hours if known | **No individual ERT is provided** — ERCOT-level rolling-blackout events are outside NRG's restoration control; agents direct customers to ERCOT's public grid-status page instead of guessing |
| Update cadence | Every 2 hours | Every 1 hour, or immediately upon any ERCOT status change |
| Safety messaging | Included if relevant | **Mandatory** on every customer contact: carbon monoxide safety (no generators/grills indoors), hypothermia risk, and NRG's bill-credit/no-disconnect commitment during the emergency window |

## 4. No-Disconnect Commitment

During any declared grid emergency, NRG suspends all service disconnections
for non-payment, regardless of the customer's account status, for the
duration of the emergency plus 5 business days after it is lifted. This
commitment applies automatically — no customer action required.

## 5. Support Agent Guidance

1. Do not speculate about restoration timing beyond what ERCOT has
   publicly stated.
2. Proactively mention the no-disconnect commitment to any customer who
   raises a billing/payment concern during an active emergency.
3. Route any customer reporting a life-threatening situation (no heat with
   a vulnerable household member, medical equipment dependency) per
   ESC-SAFETY-CONCERN immediately — do not attempt to resolve a
   safety-critical report as a standard outage inquiry.
