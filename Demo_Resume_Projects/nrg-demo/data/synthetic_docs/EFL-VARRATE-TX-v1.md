# Electricity Facts Label — VarSaver Variable Rate Plan

*This Electricity Facts Label (EFL) is provided pursuant to Public Utility
Commission of Texas (PUCT) disclosure requirements. It summarizes the
rate, fee, and contract terms of the VarSaver plan described in
PLAN-VAR-TX.*

## Electricity Price

| Average Monthly Usage | Average Price per kWh* |
|---|---|
| 500 kWh | $0.16 |
| 1,000 kWh | $0.145 |
| 2,000 kWh | $0.15 |

*Average price includes the base charge, energy charge, and applicable
TDU (transmission/distribution utility) delivery charges, at the energy
rate published at the time of this EFL's effective date. Because this is
a **variable rate plan**, the energy charge — and therefore this average
price table — may change monthly with 30 days' notice; see PLAN-VAR-TX
§2 for the update mechanism.

## Contract Summary

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Contract type | Month-to-month, no fixed term |
| Early termination fee | None |
| Renewable energy content | 0% (see Renewable Energy Content below) |
| Minimum usage fee | None |

## Renewable Energy Content

This plan's electricity is sourced at 0% from renewable resources,
compared to the ERCOT average of approximately 30% renewable generation
mix (published separately by the Public Utility Commission of Texas).

## Other Key Terms

Deposit requirements, if any, are determined by a credit check at
enrollment and disclosed separately in the Terms of Service. This EFL
does not itself impose a deposit requirement.
