# SOP-114: Deviation Escalation Criteria

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the criteria used to classify a manufacturing deviation as
Critical, Major, or Minor, and specifies the escalation and notification
requirements for each classification tier.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all deviations identified during drug substance and drug product
manufacturing at Novartis CMC facilities, including in-process deviations,
batch record discrepancies, and out-of-specification (OOS) results.

## 3. Classification Criteria

### 3.1 Critical Deviation

A deviation is classified as **Critical** if any of the following apply:

- Direct impact to patient safety, product identity, strength, purity, or
  quality (does not meet a specification tied to safety or efficacy)
- Confirmed or suspected microbial contamination in an aseptic process step
- Deviation affects a batch that has already been released or distributed
- Deviation involves falsification, data integrity violation, or unauthorized
  procedure change

**Escalation requirement:** Critical deviations must be escalated to the
Qualified Person (QP) and Site Quality Head within **4 hours** of
identification. A Level 1 investigation is required, including root cause
analysis and CAPA effectiveness check, per SOP-220.

### 3.2 Major Deviation

A deviation is classified as **Major** if any of the following apply:

- Deviation affects a process parameter outside validated range but with no
  direct, confirmed safety/efficacy impact
- Deviation affects multiple batches or a full production lot
- Deviation recurs (same root cause) within a 6-month window

**Escalation requirement:** Major deviations must be escalated to the Quality
Reviewer and CAPA Owner within **24 hours** of identification. A standard
investigation with root cause analysis is required within 10 business days.

### 3.3 Minor Deviation

A deviation is classified as **Minor** if it does not meet Critical or Major
criteria above — e.g., isolated documentation errors with no product impact,
single-instance environmental excursions within alert (not action) limits.

**Escalation requirement:** Minor deviations are logged and reviewed at the
next scheduled Quality Review meeting; no immediate escalation required.

## 4. Escalation Decision Logic

A deviation meets **Critical escalation criteria** if it satisfies **any** of
the Section 3.1 conditions. Reviewers must not average or weigh criteria —
a single Critical condition is sufficient regardless of how minor other
aspects of the deviation appear.
