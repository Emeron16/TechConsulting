# Deviation Report DEV-2201

**Date identified:** 2026-06-12
**Batch affected:** BATCH-4471 (Drug Product, Line 3)
**Reported by:** Manufacturing Operator, Shift B

## Description

During in-process monitoring of the lyophilization cycle for BATCH-4471, the
chamber pressure exceeded the validated upper control limit (150 mTorr vs.
validated range of 80-120 mTorr) for a continuous period of 22 minutes
during the primary drying phase. The excursion was detected by the
automated monitoring system and confirmed by the shift supervisor.

## Initial Assessment

- No confirmed loss of product sterility (aseptic barrier was not breached)
- Product from BATCH-4471 has **not** been released or distributed
- This is the second chamber-pressure excursion on this lyophilizer in the
  past 4 months (prior instance: DEV-2144, same root cause category pending
  confirmation)

## Preliminary Classification (pending investigation)

Per SOP-114 Section 3.1, a pressure excursion during a critical process step
(lyophilization, which is part of the aseptic fill-finish process) with
recurrence within a 6-month window is under review for Critical
classification, primarily due to the recurrence criterion and the process
step's direct link to product quality attributes.

## Status

Investigation opened under SOP-220. Root cause category not yet finalized.
