# CAPA-3390: Lyophilizer Pressure Control Valve PM Interval

## Root Cause Addressed

Equipment failure — faulty pressure control valve on Line 3 lyophilizer,
per DEV-2144 investigation (SOP-220).

## Corrective Action

Replaced the faulty pressure control valve on the Line 3 lyophilizer
(completed 2026-02-20).

## Preventive Action

Reduced preventive maintenance (PM) interval for the pressure control valve
from 12 months to 6 months, effective 2026-03-25. Updated PM schedule filed
with Facilities Engineering.

## Effectiveness Check Plan

Per SOP-301, monitoring period is 90-180 days post-implementation
(2026-03-25 to 2026-09-21). Effectiveness metric: no chamber-pressure
excursion attributable to valve malfunction on Line 3 during the monitoring
window.

## Status

Open — monitoring period in progress as of last record update. Approval
authority: Quality Reviewer/CAPA Owner (Major deviation tier per SOP-301
Section 5).
