1Purpose & Scope

This procedure defines the acceptance criteria, sampling method, and permissible hold time between final blending and tablet compression for solid oral dosage products manufactured in Suites 4A/4B, in order to ensure blend homogeneity is maintained through to compression and that content uniformity of the finished tablet is not compromised by excipient settling or de-mixing during hold.

This SOP applies to all products on the standard blend-then-compress process train. It does not apply to direct-compression formulations without a pre-blend hold step, or to continuous manufacturing lines (see SOP-140).

2Definitions

Blend Uniformity RSD

Relative standard deviation of active ingredient assay across ten stratified blend samples, expressed as a percentage.

Hold Time

Elapsed time from confirmed blend completion (final blender stop) to the start of tablet compression feed.

Action Limit

The upper boundary beyond which a result is out of specification (OOS) and the batch must be quarantined pending investigation, distinct from the routine target range which triggers a deviation but not an automatic quarantine.

4Procedure

4.1 — Blend Sampling
  1. Upon blender stop, record blend completion time in the batch record and MES.
  2. Pull ten (10) stratified samples per the current Sampling Plan (SOP-114 Attachment A), from top, middle, and bottom strata of the blend vessel.
  3. Submit samples to QA/IPC lab for active ingredient assay; do not proceed to compression pending result unless a validated rapid in-line method is qualified for this product (see product-specific Master Formula).
4.2 — Acceptance Criteria
ParameterTargetAction LimitDisposition if exceeded
Blend Uniformity RSD ≤ 3.0% 5.0% 3.0–5.0%: open deviation record, proceed pending impact assessment. >5.0%: quarantine batch, OOS investigation per SOP-201.
4.3 — Blend-to-Compression Hold Time GxP CRITICAL PARAMETER

Maximum hold time between confirmed blend completion and start of compression feed is 30 minutes (target), with an upper limit of 45 minutes before mandatory re-test of blend uniformity is required prior to compression.

  1. Production Supervisor confirms tablet press availability before initiating final blend, to minimize queue time.
  2. If projected press availability will exceed the 30-minute target at the time blending is initiated, Supervisor must escalate to reschedule press changeover or hold blend start until press is ready.
  3. If hold time reaches 30–45 minutes despite scheduling controls, this is a reportable deviation (minor) even if RSD result remains within specification, and must be logged per Section 5 below.
  4. If hold time exceeds 45 minutes, blend uniformity must be re-sampled and re-tested before compression may proceed; do not rely on the original result.
4.4 — Compression Release
  1. QA/IPC confirms blend RSD result meets acceptance criteria (Section 4.2) and hold time is within limits (Section 4.3) before authorizing compression start.
  2. Record blend release authorization (analyst initials, time) in the batch record prior to Step 6 (Compression) sign-off.

5Deviation Handling

Any excursion against Section 4.2 (RSD 3.0–5.0%) or Section 4.3 (hold time 30–45 min) must be recorded as a deviation referencing this SOP number and section, per the site Deviation Management procedure (SOP-201). The deviation record must include root cause analysis addressing why the hold-time or RSD target was not met, and a CAPA if a systemic or recurring cause (e.g., scheduling, equipment availability) is identified.

Example: a hold time of 38 minutes together with an RSD of 4.6% is within action limits on both parameters individually, but both being outside target simultaneously should be evaluated together for a shared root cause (see companion deviation record template, DEV-XXXX-XXX-A).
