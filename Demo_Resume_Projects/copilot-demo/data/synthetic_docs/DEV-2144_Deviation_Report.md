# Deviation Report DEV-2144

**Date identified:** 2026-02-18
**Batch affected:** BATCH-4108 (Drug Product, Line 3)
**Reported by:** Manufacturing Operator, Shift A

## Description

Chamber pressure on the Line 3 lyophilizer exceeded the validated upper
control limit (146 mTorr vs. validated range of 80-120 mTorr) for 15 minutes
during primary drying.

## Investigation Outcome

Root cause category: **Equipment failure or malfunction** (per SOP-220
Section 3) — a faulty pressure control valve was identified and replaced.
Investigation closed 2026-03-25 (35 days post-identification, within the
Major deviation timeliness requirement).

## Classification

Classified as **Major** per SOP-114 Section 3.2 (process parameter outside
validated range, no confirmed safety/efficacy impact, single occurrence at
time of classification).

## CAPA

CAPA-3390 opened: preventive maintenance interval for the Line 3 lyophilizer
pressure control valve reduced from 12 months to 6 months. Effectiveness
check scheduled per SOP-301 (90-180 day window for standard CAPA).
