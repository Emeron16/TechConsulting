1Description of Event

During Step 5 (final blend of extragranular excipients) of Batch Manufacturing Record BMR-77214-Rev.C, the in-process blend uniformity check returned a relative standard deviation (RSD) of 4.6% against a target specification of ≤ 3.0% RSD. The result remained within the qualified action limit of 5.0% RSD but exceeded the routine target range, triggering this deviation record per SOP-114 §6.2 (Blend Uniformity Excursion Handling).

No other in-process parameters for this batch were out of specification at the time of detection. Compression (Step 6) had not yet begun; the batch was placed on quality hold pending investigation before proceeding.

3Root Cause Analysis

Method: Structured cause-category review (Ishikawa) plus 5-Why, conducted 14–15 Aug 2024.

Method / Process
Blend-to-compression hold time reached 38 min; SOP-114 §4.3 guidance target is ≤ 30 min to limit settling/de-mixing of the extragranular blend prior to compression feed.
Man / Personnel
No procedural deviation by operators — dispensing and blend steps executed per SOP; delay originated downstream of blend completion (press changeover queue), not operator error.
Machine / Equipment
Tablet press TP-07 was completing changeover from a prior product line at time of blend completion, extending the queue time before blend could be fed to compression.
Materials
Material lots (API, MCC, Mg stearate) within specification on incoming release; no material-attributable contribution identified.
Root Cause (5-Why terminus)
Press changeover scheduling did not account for blend hold-time sensitivity — the production schedule sequenced TP-07 changeover to overlap with, rather than precede, blend completion, resulting in an 8-minute hold-time overrun beyond SOP-114 guidance.
